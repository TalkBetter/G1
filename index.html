Unit 2｜物品、遊樂場與位置

① 必背單字（12 組）

② 位置介系詞（7 個）

③ 句型整理

Where is + 單數名詞? → It is / It's + 位置描述。
Where are + 複數名詞? → They are / They're + 位置描述。
Where is the lamp? → It's on the table.
Where is the pillow? → It's on the bed.
Where are the slippers? → They're under the bed.
Where are the plants? → They're in front of the window.
⭐ 單數用 is / it；複數用 are / they。slippers、monkey bars 為複數形式。位置描述常用「介系詞 + the + 名詞」。

Unit 3｜情緒、感覺與天氣

① 情緒與感覺（12 個）

② 天氣（6 個）

③ 主詞與 be 動詞

主詞be 動詞例句
IamI am happy.
He / SheisShe is tired.
You / We / TheyareThey are hungry.

④ 句型整理

肯定句：Subject + am/is/are + adjective.
問句：Am/Is/Are + subject + adjective?
回答：Yes, subject + be verb. / No, subject + be verb + not.
Is he tired? → Yes, he is.
Is she angry? → No, she isn't. She is happy.
Are they excited? → Yes, they are.
Are you thirsty? → No, I'm not.
⭐ be 動詞要跟主詞配對；形容詞放在 be 動詞後面。回答 Are you...? 時，應使用 I am / I'm not。

Unit 4｜動作能力與臉部

③ 句型整理

What can + subject + do? → Subject + can + 原形動詞。
Can + subject + 原形動詞? → Yes, subject + can. / No, subject + can't.
What can she do? → She can swim.
Can he sing? → Yes, he can.
Can she ride a bike? → No, she can't. She can swim.
⭐ can 後面一定是動詞原形：She can sing. ✓　She can sings. ✗
